This folder is for uploading your animal sprite sheets.

Please upload the images you provided into this folder, making sure to name them exactly:
- `cat.png`
- `dog.png`
- `dolphin.png`
- `fox.png`
- `owl.png`
- `panda.png`
- `penguin.png`
- `unicorn.png`

They will be automatically picked up by your Companion!
